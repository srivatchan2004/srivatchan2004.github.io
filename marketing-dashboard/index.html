// Key Insights & Recommendations

▲ High-Performing Strategies

  • Retargeting (Cart Abandoners) delivers the best ROAS at 5.79× — every ₹1 spent returns ₹5.79.
  • Electronics Flash Sale has the highest CTR (4.1%) — product-specific urgency campaigns work well.
  • Diwali + Festive campaigns together drove ₹9.6L revenue (52% of total ad revenue).
  • 25–34 age group converts 2.3× better than 45+ — primary targeting segment confirmed.

▼ Under-Performing Areas

  • Brand Awareness Q1 ROAS is 2.67× — below the 3.5× break-even target. High impression, low intent.
  • Conversion funnel drops 86.6% at Add-to-Cart stage — pricing or UX friction likely causes cart abandonment.
  • New Customer Acquisition CTR (2.3%) is below platform average (2.9%) — creative needs refresh.
  • 45+ age group has the highest CPC (₹89) but lowest conversion rate (0.9%) — consider excluding.

💡 Strategic Recommendations

  • Reallocate 30% of Brand Awareness budget to Retargeting — highest ROI channel.
  • Launch a Q2 conversion campaign (Apr–Jun) to fill the revenue gap in the weak season.
  • A/B test new creatives for the New Customer Acquisition campaign — aim for CTR above 3%.
  • Fix cart abandonment: add urgency emails + retargeting ads within 24h of cart abandon.

⚠ Data Quality & Tracking Flags

  • 3 CTR values were inflated in Ads Manager export (formula bug) — always validate against raw clicks/impressions.
  • 2 rows had USD values instead of INR — confirm currency setting in Ads Manager account level.
  • 11 paused ad-set rows with zero metrics inflated campaign averages — filter before analysis.
  • Attribution window: data uses 7-day click / 1-day view. Consider testing 1-day click for tighter ROAS accuracy.
