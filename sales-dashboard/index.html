// Key Insights & Recommendations

▲ Growth Opportunities

  • October (Diwali) generated ₹5.21L — 27.4% margin. Replicate festive strategies in Q1.
  • Electronics drives 35% revenue and 40% profit — highest ROI category. Expand SKUs.
  • Top 3 products (Galaxy A55, AirPods Pro, HP Laptop) = 30% of total revenue. Stock heavily.
  • Repeat customers (58%) contribute 71% of revenue — loyalty programme investment advised.

▼ Problem Areas

  • June recorded a ₹-0.28L loss — COGS spiked 10% while demand dropped. Review supplier pricing.
  • Sports category margin is only 8.3% — below company avg of 23.9%. Consider pricing revision.
  • Arun T. is 12% below sales target — needs coaching or territory reassignment.
  • Chennai Outskirts region contributes only 15% despite 28% of population base — low penetration.

💡 Strategic Recommendations

  • Launch a Q2 promotion campaign in May–June to offset the consistent mid-year dip.
  • Increase Electronics & Beauty bundle offers — both categories have high margins (24%+).
  • Target Chennai Outskirts with hyper-local digital marketing — untapped 15% addressable market.
  • Introduce referral incentives for repeat customers to accelerate new customer acquisition.

⚠ Data Quality Flags

  • 14 duplicate order IDs found (possibly double-entries in CRM). Recommend audit of order entry process.
  • COGS data missing for 5 orders — estimated from category avg. Finance team to confirm actuals.
  • 3 orders show negative quantities — likely return/refund entries logged incorrectly. Fix data pipeline.
  • Date format inconsistency across 6 rows — suggests multi-source data entry. Standardize form inputs.
